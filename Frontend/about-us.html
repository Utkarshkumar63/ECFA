About ECFA

Know Our Legacy, Vision & Mission

Our History

The East Champaran Fencing Association was established to promote fencing as a competitive sport in the region. Since our inception, we have trained hundreds of fencers and organized numerous state and national level tournaments.

Our association has been instrumental in identifying young talent and providing them with world-class training facilities and coaching.

🎯 Our Vision

To establish East Champaran as a hub for fencing excellence in India and develop athletes who can represent the nation at international platforms.

🚀 Our Mission

  • Promote fencing as a sport in the region
  • Identify and nurture young fencing talent
  • Provide quality training and coaching
  • Organize competitive tournaments
  • Create opportunities for national and international participation

Our Objectives

1. Talent Development

Identify and develop young fencing talent through structured training programs.

2. Training Excellence

Provide world-class training under experienced coaches and instructors.

3. Competition

Organize tournaments at district, state, and national levels.

4. Community Building

Foster a strong fencing community in East Champaran.
