About ECFA

Our legacy, vision, and mission

Our History

East Champaran Fencing Association (ECFA) was established with the aim of promoting and developing the sport of fencing in the East Champaran district of Bihar. Since its inception, the association has been actively working to create awareness about fencing, organize competitions, and nurture talented players at the district level. It serves as the official governing body for fencing in the region, ensuring proper structure, growth, and representation of athletes at state and national levels.

🎯 Our Vision

Our vision is to make East Champaran a recognized hub for fencing excellence by nurturing young talent and enabling them to compete at state, national, and international levels. We aspire to uphold the values of sportsmanship, Olympism, and fair play while contributing to the overall development of sports in the region.

🚀 Our Mission

  • To grow and promote fencing in East Champaran
  • To provide training and opportunities for young players
  • To support athletes in reaching state and national levels
  • To maintain discipline, respect, and true sportsmanship
  • To build a strong and supportive fencing community

Our Objectives

1. Talent Development

To identify and develop young fencing talent through structured training programs and continuous support.

2. Training Excellence

To promote, develop and popularize the sport of fencing in East Champaran, Motihari district of Bihar.

3. Competition

To establish rules, regulations, and byelaws for the smooth conduct of fencing activities and competitions, and update them when required.

4. Community Building

To affiliate with the State Fencing Association and follow their rules, regulations, and guidelines while strengthening the local fencing community.
